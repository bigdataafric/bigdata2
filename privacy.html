Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data protection are very important to us at Big Data Consultancy Ltd.

1. Data Protection & Privacy Policy

Big Data Consultancy Ltd. (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information..

2. Definitions

1. Definitions In order to clearly understand how we handle information at Big Data Consultancy Ltd., it is important to define key terms that are frequently used throughout this Privacy Policy. These definitions ensure transparency and help our clients, collaborators, and website visitors comprehend the scope of data we process, the level of protection applied, and the responsibilities we uphold in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Personal Data refers to any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual. This may include, but is not limited to, a person’s name, email address, phone number, physical address, or professional details such as job title or organization. Personal data is the fundamental building block of our consultancy services, as it allows us to communicate effectively with clients, understand their project requirements, and deliver research and consultancy solutions tailored to their needs. At Big Data Consultancy Ltd., we are committed to collecting personal data in a lawful, fair, and transparent manner, ensuring that all individuals providing their information are aware of how it will be used. Special Category Data refers to sensitive personal information that requires a higher level of protection due to its nature. This includes data related to an individual’s health, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, or other sensitive identifiers. In our work, special category data may arise in research projects such as the Prediction of Caesarean Section in Pregnant Women or Diabetes Research, where personal health data is crucial for accurate analysis and decision-making. Because this data is highly sensitive, Big Data Consultancy Ltd. takes additional measures to ensure its security, including explicit consent from individuals, secure storage, restricted access, and compliance with all relevant data protection regulations. Processing encompasses any operation or set of operations performed on personal data, whether automated or manual. This includes collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, dissemination, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure, or destruction of data. In the context of Big Data Consultancy Ltd., processing allows us to transform raw data into meaningful insights that inform our consultancy services, research outputs, and recommendations to clients. We ensure that all processing activities are conducted with integrity, confidentiality, and in strict compliance with legal obligations, while also maintaining transparency with data subjects regarding the purpose and scope of processing. By establishing clear definitions for personal data, special category data, and processing, Big Data Consultancy Ltd. demonstrates its commitment to ethical, secure, and responsible management of information. These definitions provide the foundation for all data handling practices across our services, ensuring that clients and stakeholders can trust that their information is treated with the highest standards of privacy and security..

3. General Principles

At Big Data Consultancy Ltd., the protection of personal and sensitive information is central to our operations. We adhere to the general principles of data protection as required by Ghanaian law and best practice standards in information security. Data is collected and processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently. Each piece of information we handle serves a clear purpose and is limited to what is necessary for delivering our consultancy and research services. We ensure that all data we collect is accurate, kept up-to-date, and stored in a manner that prevents unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Our approach to data management is guided by the principles of accountability and responsibility, ensuring that all staff members and collaborators understand their role in safeguarding client and research information. These principles form the foundation of trust between Big Data Consultancy Ltd. and our clients, partners, and stakeholders..

4. Business Activities Where We Collect Personal Data

Personal data is collected as part of our professional consultancy services and research activities. This includes, but is not limited to, interactions with clients seeking advice, conducting feasibility studies, preparing research proposals, or delivering training programs. 5. Business Activities Where We Collect Special Category Data

Obtaining consent is a fundamental aspect of our data protection practice. At Big Data Consultancy Ltd., consent is sought whenever personal or sensitive data is collected. Consent may be obtained through digital forms, signed agreements, email confirmations, or verbal agreement in direct consultancy engagements. We ensure that consent is informed, specific, and freely given, allowing clients or research participants to fully understand the purposes for which their data will be processed.
